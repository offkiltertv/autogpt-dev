Scope: 90-day launch pacing based on current Arcana inventory baseline.

## Scenario Planning

## Scenario A: 1/day
- 90-day output: 90
- Remaining Arcana backlog after 90 days: 1,257
- Total backlog duration at steady pace: 1,347 days (~3.69 years)
- Use when:
  - minimal moderation staff
  - quality control is primary concern
  - slow-build SEO strategy

## Scenario B: 3/day
- 90-day output: 270
- Remaining Arcana backlog after 90 days: 1,077
- Total backlog duration at steady pace: 449 days (~1.23 years)
- Use when:
  - moderation is stable
  - forum team can handle daily thread volume
  - growth target is balanced traffic + engagement

## Scenario C: 5/day
- 90-day output: 450
- Remaining Arcana backlog after 90 days: 897
- Total backlog duration at steady pace: 270 days (~9 months)
- Use when:
  - operations and moderation capacity are strong
  - objective is rapid market presence
  - acceptable risk of audience fatigue is managed

## Recommended 90-Day Cadence
Adopt phased pacing instead of fixed high volume:

Phase 1 (Days 1-14)
- Arcana: 2/day
- Focus: process stability, moderation calibration, QA loop

Phase 2 (Days 15-45)
- Arcana: 3/day weekdays, 1/day weekends
- Focus: momentum + forum growth without oversaturation

Phase 3 (Days 46-90)
- Arcana: 2/day weekdays, 1/day weekends + 1 weekly recap thread
- Focus: retention and discussion quality

Projected Arcana output for phased model:
- Days 1-14: 28
- Days 15-45: 117
- Days 46-90: 99
- 90-day total: 244
- Remaining backlog: 1,103

## Content Pacing Rules
1. No more than 3 Arcana posts in one calendar day unless forum response rate >20%.
2. Maintain minimum 1 Arcana post/day for continuity.
3. Schedule highest-quality Arcana entries in prime windows (late morning + evening local).
4. Insert one weekly “Arcana Recap” linking top discussions to improve return visits.

## Decision Guidance
- Choose 1/day if reliability and moderation are constrained.
- Choose 3/day if growth and quality balance is the goal.
- Use 5/day only as a short campaign burst, not default steady-state.
