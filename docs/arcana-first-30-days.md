# Arcana First 30 Days

Date: 2026-06-13
Inventory baseline: 1327 videos

## Cadence Comparison (Total Arcana posts/day)

1/day
- Lifespan: 1327 days (~43.6 months)
- Pros: maximum longevity, slow moderation load
- Cons: slower homepage/search velocity

4/day
- Lifespan: 331.75 days (~10.9 months)
- Pros: strong freshness + manageable operations
- Cons: faster inventory burn than low cadence

24/day
- Lifespan: 55.29 days (~1.82 months)
- Pros: fastest index expansion and feed turnover
- Cons: high fatigue risk, high moderation burden, rapid inventory depletion

## Recommended Initial Production Cadence
Recommendation: **4/day total** for first 30 days.

Why:
- Meaningful daily freshness for homepage and archive visitors
- Stronger SEO crawl/index signal than 1/day
- Still sustainable vs. 24/day burn rate

## 30-Day Operating Plan

Week 1 (Stabilize)
- Publish at 4/day total.
- Confirm daily import health for campaigns `8089` and `8090`.
- Confirm Arcana section and menu are live.
- Validate all four discovery surfaces daily:
  - Homepage Arcana block
  - Arcana archive
  - Premonitions/Outcomes archives
  - Search

Week 2 (Optimize Discovery)
- Tune Arcana homepage block order/count if CTR is weak.
- Add/update sidebar Arcana entry point.
- Continue 4/day unless errors or moderation backlog appears.

Week 3 (SEO Consolidation)
- Ensure internal linking from homepage/menu/sidebar to Arcana archives.
- Monitor indexed pages and search impressions/clicks.
- Maintain clean taxonomy consistency on new imports.

Week 4 (Scale Decision)
- If engagement is healthy and moderation stable:
  - keep 4/day, or test controlled increase.
- If fatigue/moderation pressure appears:
  - reduce to 2/day or 1/day temporarily.

## Daily Operator Checklist
1. Campaigns `8089` and `8090` executed successfully.
2. New Arcana posts published in expected cadence window.
3. New posts appear in Arcana archives and search.
4. Homepage Arcana block populated.
5. No repeated import errors/duplicate anomalies.

## Success Metrics (First 30 Days)
- Content velocity: target 120 Arcana posts in 30 days (at 4/day).
- Homepage visibility: Arcana section always populated.
- User discovery: Arcana archive traffic and menu click-through rising.
- SEO indexing: steady growth in indexed Arcana video URLs.

## Risk Controls
- Keep imports as normal posts (not forced-featured globally).
- Use section-level discovery rather than sticky behavior.
- If quality drops, lower cadence before expanding sources.
