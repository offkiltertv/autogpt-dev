## Pilot Success Metrics

### Primary success criteria
1. Creator registers account.
2. Claim verification completed.
3. Channel/profile linked to creator account.
4. Creator updates at least 2 profile fields (avatar + bio minimum).
5. Creator performs at least one community action (comment or forum participation).

### Secondary success criteria
1. At least one Arcana post correctly associated to claimed creator identity.
2. No playback or visibility regression after ownership handoff.
3. Claim cycle time <= 5 business days end-to-end.

## Pilot Outcome States
- **Success**: all 5 primary criteria met.
- **Partial Success**: 3-4 primary criteria met, no regressions.
- **Fail / Retry**: fewer than 3 primary criteria met, or ownership regression occurs.

## Decision Gate For Wave 2
Proceed to broader outreach only if:
1. Wave 1 reaches Success or strong Partial Success.
2. Manual playbook steps are reproducible without incident.
3. Ownership mapping policy is clear and documented for staff.
