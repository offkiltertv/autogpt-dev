# OFFKILTER Pulse — Editorial Guidelines

Date: 2026-06-29
Status: v1 (Platform v2.2)
Audience: creators, editors, operators, moderators.

---

## What Pulse is

Pulse is OFFKILTER's **short-form discovery layer**. It is where fast, current,
human moments surface and get found.

Pulse **is**:
- **Fast observations** — something noticed, framed, and shared quickly.
- **Breaking developments** — the new thing, while it's still new.
- **Quick creator updates** — a creator speaking directly to their audience.
- **Transformative commentary** — reaction, criticism, education, parody — value *added*.
- **Short-form discovery** — a way in, not the whole story.

## What Pulse is NOT

Pulse is **not** "Shorts," "Reels," or "TikToks." Those are formats optimized for
infinite passive consumption and raw redistribution. Pulse is the opposite posture:

- Not raw redistribution — every Pulse should **add something** (a take, context, a frame).
- Not an endless dopamine feed — Pulse is **editorial and discovery-first**.
- Not anonymous virality — Pulse is **creator-attributed** and community-connected.
- Not a clone of another platform's format — Pulse is **unmistakably OFFKILTER**.

> If a clip could be posted identically on three other apps with nothing added,
> it isn't Pulse — it's redistribution.

---

## The transformation test

Every Pulse should pass at least one:
- **Commentary** — you say something about it.
- **Criticism** — you evaluate or critique it.
- **Education** — you teach with it.
- **Parody / reaction** — you transform its meaning.
- **Original observation** — it's your own captured moment, framed.

The Pulse Clipper (standalone app) biases toward this: trim → annotate → voice/text →
publish. The tool makes "adding something" the path of least resistance.

---

## Quality bar (what gets featured)

| Tier | Bar |
|---|---|
| **Featured Pulse** (editor's pick) | Strong transformation + clear creator voice + timely or insightful. Hand-picked. |
| **Trending Pulse** | Earning genuine engagement (discussion, returns) — not just views. |
| **Latest Pulse** | Meets the transformation test; published and `ready`. |
| **Not surfaced** | Raw redistribution, low-effort reposts, or anything failing the transformation test. |

---

## Voice & framing

- Lead with the creator and the **why-watch**, not the source.
- Titles are observations, not clickbait ("What everyone missed about X", not "YOU WON'T BELIEVE").
- Source attribution is **honest and optional** — credit where the moment came from; never fabricate.
- Respect copyright. Encourage transformative use; never imply OFFKILTER determines fair use.

---

## Lifecycle & states

`processing → ready → (featured / trending / latest)`; visibility `private | unlisted | public`.

- **Processing**: just uploaded from the Pulse Clipper; not yet public. Only the owner sees it (with a "Processing…" badge).
- **Ready**: public-surface eligible.
- A Pulse can be **promoted** (featured) editorially at any time.

---

## Moderation principles

- Remove raw redistribution and TOS violations; keep the bar high but the process humane.
- Creator opt-out is always honored (see `docs/creator-optout.md`).
- A reserved **Discuss** thread (SidebarChat, future) accompanies every Pulse — community is part of the product, not a bolt-on.

---

## How Pulse relates to the other destinations

See `docs/offkilter-platform-roadmap.md` → Discovery Engine. In short: **Pulse** is
short-form/current, **Signals** is the standout-clip highlight reel, **Arcana** is
the intuitive vertical, **Creators** is the people. They complement; they don't compete.
