# Content Priority Matrix

Date: 2026-06-13
Purpose: Ranking model for editorial focus, traffic growth, and monetization readiness.

## Priority Ranking

Priority 1: Arcana
- Why:
  - Core brand differentiator
  - Deep discussion potential (forum-first)
  - High return-visitor potential
  - Strong membership upsell fit

Priority 2: Commentary
- Why:
  - High publishing flexibility
  - Fast response to trends and current conversation
  - Reliable ad inventory support
  - Bridges broad audience into Arcana and Investigations

Priority 3: Reality TV
- Why:
  - Conversation-heavy audience behavior
  - Recap/prediction format drives repeat thread activity
  - Good cadence balance for non-Arcana days

Priority 4: Gaming
- Why:
  - Discovery traffic potential
  - Community interaction opportunities
  - Good sponsor alignment for creator/gaming tooling

Priority 5: Investigations
- Why:
  - High-depth, high-trust content
  - Strong long-session behavior
  - Slower production velocity; needs tighter QA

Priority 6: Music
- Why:
  - Useful for variety and session loops
  - Lower forum-discussion depth relative to top priorities
  - Better as supporting lane than primary growth lane

## Execution Guidance by Priority
1. Publish Priority 1 + 2 every week without fail.
2. Maintain weekly minimums for Priority 3 + 4.
3. Use Priority 5 + 6 strategically for depth and diversity.

## Resource Allocation (Editor Time)
- Arcana: 35%
- Commentary: 20%
- Reality TV: 15%
- Gaming: 12%
- Investigations: 12%
- Music: 6%

## Escalation Rule
If any lower-priority silo outperforms Arcana/Commentary on 4-week rolling KPIs (returns, forum replies, RPM), temporarily raise its slot share by 10-20% for a 2-week test.
