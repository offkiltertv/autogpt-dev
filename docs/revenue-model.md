# Revenue Model

Date: 2026-06-13
Purpose: Practical monetization model by content silo for OffKilter execution.

## Revenue Streams
1. AdSense (page views, session depth, returning traffic)
2. Memberships (ARMember tiers, premium discussions, gated archives)
3. Sponsorships (silo-specific brand alignment)
4. Forum engagement monetization (retention, conversions, community value)

## Silo Monetization Mapping

## Arcana
- AdSense opportunity: High (longer session depth + related entry loops)
- Membership opportunity: Very High (premium interpretations, private threads, archive access)
- Sponsorship opportunity: Medium-High (books, learning tools, mindfulness/self-development)
- Forum opportunity: Very High (discussion-first format)

## Commentary
- AdSense opportunity: High (consistent cadence, trend-driven views)
- Membership opportunity: Medium (premium analysis digests)
- Sponsorship opportunity: Medium (media/productivity/news tools)
- Forum opportunity: High (debate and opinion threads)

## Reality TV
- AdSense opportunity: Medium-High (recap and event spikes)
- Membership opportunity: Medium (member recaps/predictions)
- Sponsorship opportunity: Medium (lifestyle/entertainment)
- Forum opportunity: High (episodic conversation loops)

## Gaming
- AdSense opportunity: Medium (discovery + recurring interest)
- Membership opportunity: Medium (guides, curated picks)
- Sponsorship opportunity: High (gear, creator tools, gaming services)
- Forum opportunity: Medium-High (strategy/meta discussions)

## Investigations
- AdSense opportunity: Medium-High (long-form dwell time)
- Membership opportunity: High (dossiers, evidence packs, premium threads)
- Sponsorship opportunity: Medium (research/productivity/case tools)
- Forum opportunity: Medium-High (evidence-focused discussion)

## Music
- AdSense opportunity: Medium (playlist browsing and discovery pages)
- Membership opportunity: Low-Medium (curated collections)
- Sponsorship opportunity: Medium (audio software/hardware)
- Forum opportunity: Medium (recommendation threads)

## Technology
- AdSense opportunity: Medium-High (evergreen search traffic)
- Membership opportunity: Medium (toolkits/how-to packs)
- Sponsorship opportunity: High (SaaS/dev tools)
- Forum opportunity: Medium (Q&A/help threads)

## Streaming
- AdSense opportunity: Medium (recurring publish rhythm)
- Membership opportunity: Medium (behind-the-scenes access)
- Sponsorship opportunity: Medium (creator infra)
- Forum opportunity: Medium-High (live-thread recaps)

## Revenue Sequencing (First 90 Days)
1. Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): prioritize AdSense-ready page output + forum seeding.
2. Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): launch first membership hooks in Arcana and Investigations.
3. Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): package sponsorship one-sheets by top 2 performing silos.

## Minimum KPI Thresholds Before Scaling Spend
1. Return visitor rate > 20%
2. Forum reply rate > 15% on new posts
3. Ad session depth stable over 4 weeks
4. Membership conversion baseline established on Arcana pages

## Practical Revenue Focus by Priority
1. Arcana: retention + membership + depth-based ad yield
2. Commentary: cadence-based ad volume
3. Investigations: trust-based membership and premium positioning
4. Gaming/Reality/Music: audience diversification and sponsor packaging

This model balances near-term ad revenue with medium-term membership and sponsorship growth.
